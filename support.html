Support

How can we help?

The quickest answers live in the app: tap the ? on Home to ask DearHelp, which knows how everything works. Here are the questions we hear most.

How do my partner and I pair up?

After you sign in, create your universe and share your 8-character invite code (or link) with your partner, or enter the code your partner sent you.

Codes last 48 hours and work once. If one expires, open the invite screen and tap “Make a new code”.

My sign-in code didn’t arrive

There are no passwords: choose Continue with Apple (iPhone and iPad), Continue with Google, or type your email and we send you a 6-digit code.

If the code doesn’t arrive, check your spam or junk folder and that the address is right, then tap “Send a new code”.

How do I add a widget?

On iPhone, touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget and search for Dearloop. Lock Screen widgets are added the same way from the Lock Screen. In the app, Play → Widgets shows them all.

Widgets never show anything private.

Does my partner need Plus too?

No. One Plus subscription covers you both. Plus is optional; prices are shown in the app before you buy.

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store or Google Play: on the Plus page, tap “Manage or cancel”. New phone? Tap “Restore purchases” on the Plus page. If Plus ends, nothing is deleted.

What are Flirty & Suggestive and After Dark?

Two optional question decks for adults. Both of you have to switch Flirty & Suggestive on, and each of you confirms you’re 18 or older. After Dark needs both of you to switch it on too. Until you both have, they stay closed.

Either of you can switch a section off any time, and it hides straight away for both of you. Notifications, widgets and the Lock Screen never show any of it.

Can I save a copy of everything?

Yes. Memories → Keep a copy → “Download a copy of everything” makes a page of your memories, answers, letters, love drawings, notes and dates that you can save, share or print.

How do I unpair?

Us → Unpair. Your shared universe closes for both of you. You choose to keep a private copy of your memories or remove what you made, and your partner can save theirs for 30 days.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Us → scroll to the bottom → “Delete my account”, then confirm. It can’t be undone. If you have Plus, cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account too.

Without the app, see Delete your account.

Is Dearloop end-to-end encrypted?

No, and we’d rather be clear about it. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and only you and your partner can see your shared things (the database itself enforces it). We never sell your data or share what you write.

Too many notifications, or some at night?

Us → Notifications lets you choose which ones you get, how often, and your quiet hours (10:30 pm to 7:30 am by default, in your own time zone). Anything during quiet hours waits until morning.

Still stuck?

Email us from the address you sign in with and tell us what happened. We usually reply within a few days.
