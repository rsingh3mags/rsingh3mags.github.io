# Delete your Dearloop account

_Last updated 7 November 2026_

You can delete your Dearloop account and its data at any time, in the app or by email. Dearloop is operated by Rishi Singh.

## In the app (quickest)

Open Dearloop, go to the Us tab, tap Delete my account and confirm. It happens straight away. If you have Dearloop Plus, also cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions, because deleting your account doesn't stop the store from billing you.

## Without the app

Email dearloop.help@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll reply to confirm it's you, then delete your account within 30 days (usually much sooner) and tell you when it's done.

## What is deleted

Straight away: your sign-in, profile, avatar, settings, location (if you shared it), your own saved copies, the notes, letters, photos, memories and date ideas you made, and the questions you asked your partner, every answer you wrote to one, and your 18+ confirmation. Your shared universe closes for both of you, and the rest of it is deleted 30 days later. Backups roll over within 30 days.

## What may stay

If your partner chose to keep a private copy of your time together, it stays with them (including the photos it shows) until they delete it. Purchase records kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat follow their own rules. We keep nothing else.
