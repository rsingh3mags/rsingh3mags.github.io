Dearloop is a private space for two people. This policy explains what we collect, why, where it lives and the choices you have. In short: your shared space is visible only to you and your partner, we never sell your data or share what you write, the free version shows optional short videos from Google AdMob that you choose to watch for rewards (Dearloop Plus has no ads), and you can export or delete your data at any time.

Who we are

Dearloop is operated by Rishi Singh ("we"). Contact us at dearloop.help@gmail.com about anything in this policy, including requests to access, correct, export or delete your data.

What you give us

Your email address (to sign in with a one-time code). If you choose Continue with Apple or Continue with Google instead, we receive only what they share: your name and email address (Apple lets you hide your real email, and then we get a private relay address that forwards to you; Google's basic profile also includes a link to your profile picture, which we don't use or show). We use that name only to suggest what your partner calls you, and you can change it. We never get your Apple or Google password, contacts or anything else from those accounts. Then: the name and avatar you choose; your time zone; your relationship details if you add them (how you're together, the date you started dating, countdowns); and everything you create together: answers (including any photos or voice notes you attach), questions you ask each other and your answers to them (including the optional Flirty & Suggestive and After Dark sections, which sections you've switched on, and when you confirmed you're 18 or older), notes and drawings, letters, photos, memories, moods and statuses, wishes, check-ins, date ideas, your pets' names, garden, games and Stardust activity.

Your location (only if you turn it on)

"How far apart" is off until you turn it on. If you do, we store your position rounded to about 10 km and a place name (a city you pick, or one your phone suggests). Your partner sees the distance between you and that place name; nobody else does. If you share islands with more than one person, only the people in the islands where you turned it on see it. Your phone's own map service (Apple's or Google's) may be used to suggest the place name. We never track your location in the background. Turn it off at any time and we delete it.

What we collect automatically

Push notification tokens if you allow notifications; basic technical logs kept by our hosting provider (such as request times and error codes) for security and reliability; and an internal account id that links your data together. If crash reporting is enabled in your build, crash reports include device model, OS version and the error, never your messages or answers. We don't use third-party analytics SDKs.

Ads (free version only)

The free version of Dearloop has optional rewarded videos: you tap a "Watch" button to get Stardust or a pet snack. They're served by Google AdMob as non-personalised ads, after Google's consent message where the law requires it. There are no banner or pop-up ads, and nothing that helps you talk to your partner is ever behind an ad. When you watch, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects technical information to show the ad, measure it and prevent fraud, such as your device type, app version, IP address (which suggests a rough location), how you interact with the ad, diagnostics and device identifiers, plus a one-time code so our server can confirm the reward. Google processes this under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). We don't send Google your name, email, account id or anything you write, we don't use your advertising ID (it is switched off in our Android app), and we don't track you across other companies' apps. If you never tap a Watch button, the ads SDK never starts. Dearloop Plus has no ads.

Who can see your content

Only you and your partner, while you're paired. This is enforced by the database itself (row-level security), not only by the app. Answers to the daily question (including attached photos and voice notes) stay hidden from your partner until you've both answered, unless you both turn on early reveal. Answers to questions you ask each other, and to cards from the Flirty & Suggestive or After Dark decks, also stay hidden until you've both answered (or, for a question you asked only your partner, until they answer). Those two sections only open while both of you have switched them on, and either of you switching one off hides it again for both of you straight away. Letters scheduled for later can't be read by the recipient until they arrive. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show only gentle status (for example "answer waiting", a countdown or your partner's mood), never the content of answers, notes or letters; on iPhone, countdown names, place names and your partner's mood and status are hidden while the phone is locked. Notifications never include what you or your partner wrote; for questions you ask each other and the Flirty & Suggestive and After Dark sections they only ever say that your partner asked or answered something.

How it's protected

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by our infrastructure provider. Photos and voice notes are stored in private storage and shown through short-lived links. Dearloop is not end-to-end encrypted: our systems could technically access stored content, and we only do so when you ask us to (for example, for support you request) or when the law requires it.

Service providers

Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage and realtime updates; hosted in the region we chose for the project); Apple and Google, only if you choose Continue with Apple or Continue with Google (they confirm it's you and share your name and email with us, and they know you use Dearloop); Expo (delivering push notifications through Apple and Google); Apple App Store and Google Play (purchases, if you choose Dearloop Plus) and RevenueCat (confirming subscription status: it receives your account id and purchase details, never your content); Google AdMob (the optional videos in the free version, as described above); and, if enabled, Sentry (crash reports). Apart from Google's own use of ad data described above, they process data only to provide their service to us.

Why we use your data

To run the service you asked for (performance of a contract), to keep it safe and working and to fund the free version with optional ads (legitimate interests, and consent where the law requires it for ads), and to send notifications you've allowed (consent, which you can withdraw in Settings or in the app's notification preferences).

How long we keep it

While your account exists. If you unpair, your shared universe closes for both of you and is deleted 30 days later; during those 30 days each of you can choose to keep a private copy. Saved copies stay until you delete them. If you delete your account, your sign-in, profile, settings and your own saved copies are deleted straight away, the notes, letters, photos, memories and date ideas you made are removed, and the rest of your shared universe is deleted 30 days later. If your partner keeps a private copy of your time together, it stays with them (including the photos it shows) until they delete it. Location you shared is deleted with your account. Backups roll over within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

In the app you can: export your shared data, turn location sharing off, unpair (keeping a copy or removing what you made), delete saved copies, change notification preferences and quiet hours, change your ad consent (Us, then Ad privacy choices, where Google's consent message applies), and delete your account (Us, then Delete my account). Without the app, email dearloop.help@gmail.com from the address you sign in with and ask us to delete your account. Depending on where you live you may also have rights to access, correct, restrict or object to processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact dearloop.help@gmail.com.

Children

Dearloop is for adults aged 18 and over: it includes optional flirty and intimate (After Dark) questions, which both partners must switch on, and After Dark also asks each of you to confirm you're 18 or older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and if we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18 we delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
