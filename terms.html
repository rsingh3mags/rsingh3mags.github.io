Your content

What you create stays yours. You give us permission to store it and show it to you and your partner, only so we can run Dearloop. Don't upload anything you don't have the right to share, or anything illegal, abusive or that harms someone else.

Being kind

Dearloop is for people who both want to be there. Don't use it to monitor, pressure or harass anyone. The Flirty & Suggestive and After Dark sections are only for two consenting adults who have both switched them on: never use them to pressure your partner, and remember either of you can switch them off, skip a card or say no at any time. Either of you can unpair at any time.

Stardust

Stardust is a playful, in-app reward with no money value. It can't be bought, sold, transferred or exchanged for money, and we may adjust how it's earned.

Dearloop Plus

Plus is an optional subscription sold through the App Store or Google Play. Prices are shown before you buy. It renews automatically until you cancel in your store account settings at least 24 hours before the current period ends. If the store offers you a free trial, the offer says how long it lasts and what you'll pay after it; the subscription begins when the trial ends unless you cancel in your store account settings at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Dearloop reminds you in the app two days before (and with a notification if you allow them). Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies. One subscription covers one world, the one the person who pays chooses: both people in that world have Plus there, and the payer's other worlds are on Free unless they have their own Plus. The payer can move it to another of their worlds (after a move, it stays for 7 days before it can move again). If you unpair, it stays with whoever pays and moves to their main world. Plus items step aside if Plus ends, and nothing you made is deleted.

Optional videos

In the free version you can choose to watch a short video to get Stardust, a pet snack or soap, or more question cards for the day (the free version includes a number of question cards each day, shared by every deck). The reward is given once our server has confirmed the video with Google, only for videos watched to the end, and there are limits against automated use that we may change. Test versions of the app show Google's test videos. Videos are never required to talk to your partner. Dearloop Plus never needs a video: it has unlimited question cards and no ads unless you choose to watch one, for the same rewards.
